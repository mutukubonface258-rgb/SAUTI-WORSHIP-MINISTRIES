Here is your word of exhortation Leviticus 26:41, 42 I [God] also was acting with hostility against them, to bring them into the land of their enemies — or if their uncircumcised heart becomes humbled so that they then make amends for their iniquity/sins, [42] then I will remember My covenant with Jacob, and I will remember also My covenant with Abraham as well, and I will remember the land. (NASB) ~ It has been about 3,500 years since God directed Moses to write this about the Israelites. Time after time, they turned away from following Him, then repented and returned. God is amazingly forgiving if we just humble ourselves and ask for forgiveness, then redirect our lives to follow only His ways. Israel is still in this cyclical process, and God is still waiting and willing to forgive. We can learn from this pattern to stay obedient to Him throughout our lives, as we are able, then He may grant us favor if it is His desire. Have a blessed day ahead 🙏🙏🙏
